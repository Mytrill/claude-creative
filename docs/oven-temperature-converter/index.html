FAQ

Why do fan ovens use lower temperatures?
The fan circulates hot air, transferring heat faster. The rule of thumb: set a fan oven 20 °C (about 25–50 °F) lower than the conventional temperature in the recipe.
My oven only shows Fahrenheit — is 350 °F the same as 180 °C?
350 °F is 177 °C; recipes round it to 180 °C. The 3-degree difference doesn't matter in practice.
