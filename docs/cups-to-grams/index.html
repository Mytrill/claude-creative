FAQ

Why is 1 cup of flour only 120 g but 1 cup of sugar 200 g?
Density. Sugar crystals pack tightly; flour traps air. Same volume, different weight — that's why gram measurements make baking more reliable.
US cup or metric cup?
This tool uses the US cup (236.6 ml). A metric cup is 250 ml — multiply results by 1.06 if your recipe uses metric cups.
Scooped or spooned flour?
Values assume flour spooned into the cup and leveled. Scooping directly packs the flour and can add 20–30 g per cup.
