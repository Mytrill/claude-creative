FAQ

Does baking time change?
If the batter ends up shallower it bakes faster; deeper bakes slower. Start checking 10 minutes early, or drop the oven ~15 °C / 25 °F for much deeper batter.
What about loaf, bundt or springform pans?
Those depend on volume, not just area. A springform converts like a regular round pan of the same diameter; loaf and bundt pans don't convert reliably by area — match volume with water if you can.
