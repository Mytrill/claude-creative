How it works

Each line's leading quantity ("2", "1 1/2", "½", "0.75") is multiplied by your scaling factor and rounded to a sensible fraction. Lines without a number (like "salt to taste") pass through unchanged. Tip: eggs and leaveners don't always scale linearly — round eggs to whole numbers and taste as you go.

FAQ

Can I halve a recipe?
Yes — set "recipe serves" to 2 and "I want to serve" to 1 (or 4 → 2). Any ratio works, including thirds.
Does doubling change baking time?
Not if you use two pans of the original size. One bigger, deeper pan bakes slower — check early and often. Our cake pan converter helps pick the pan.
