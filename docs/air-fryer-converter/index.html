Oven to air fryer converter

An air fryer is a small, fast convection oven: same food needs a lower temperature and less time.

How the conversion works

The standard rule: reduce the temperature by 15 °C (25 °F) and the cooking time by about 20%. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes before the timer ends, and shake or flip halfway. Preheating is usually unnecessary. Don't overcrowd the basket — air needs to circulate.

FAQ

Does this work for baking?
Cookies and small bakes, yes. Cakes and bread are riskier — the intense fan can set the top before the middle cooks. Use the lower end of the temperature and test with a skewer.
Frozen food bags already list air fryer times — which wins?
The bag wins. Use this converter when you only have oven instructions.
